ANSWERS TO COMMENTS AND FEEDBACK FOR BAKEWISE PROPOSAL

This document provides structured answers to the panel's comments and feedback based on the actual implementation of the BakeWise system prototype (as coded in `app.js`).

1. Strengthen the justification for using Artificial Intelligence.
- Why is Artificial Intelligence necessary for this system?
Answer: AI and predictive analytics are necessary to quickly transform historical operational data into actionable insights, enabling rapid demand forecasting, automated inventory optimization, and proactive waste reduction that manual estimation cannot achieve efficiently.
- What specific machine learning algorithm will be used?
Answer: In the developed system prototype, the "AI" demand forecasting is implemented as a customized Time-Series Moving Average combined with a cyclical time-based variance coefficient to simulate dynamic market fluctuations. 
- Why was that algorithm chosen over other forecasting techniques?
Answer: This lightweight, deterministic algorithm was chosen for the prototype because it can perform real-time calculations directly in the application without requiring a heavy backend infrastructure. It provides immediate, responsive forecasts based on the most recent short-term trends.

2. Clarify the AI implementation.
- What machine learning model will be used?
Answer: The system uses a Custom Moving Average algorithm multiplied by a sinusoidal variance coefficient.
- How will the model be trained?
Answer: Instead of a traditional model training phase, the algorithm dynamically calculates predictions in real-time by querying the active sales database for the latest records.
- What historical data will serve as the training dataset?
Answer: The forecasting algorithm specifically retrieves and analyzes the last 3 recorded sales transactions for each specific product to determine recent demand trends.
- How much historical data is required before the AI can generate reliable predictions?
Answer: The algorithm is designed to work with very limited data, requiring a minimum of 1 to 3 recent sales records to generate a baseline moving average prediction.
- How will the accuracy of the AI model be evaluated?
Answer: The accuracy will be evaluated by comparing the system's generated daily forecast against the actual recorded sales for that day during the testing phase.

3. Shelf-life prediction needs further explanation.
- Since bakery products already have known shelf-life durations, what exactly is the AI predicting? / Is it predicting actual spoilage based on storage conditions, or simply calculating expiration dates?
Answer: The system is calculating expiration timelines rather than predicting physical spoilage based on environmental factors. The system calculates a "Freshness Index" percentage based strictly on the production date and the known expiration date. When the freshness drops to 30% or below, the system automatically flags the item for action.

4. Product repurposing recommendations require justification.
- Are these recommendations AI-generated or predefined by the system?
Answer: The repurposing recommendations are predefined by rule-based logic. Each product in the database is assigned a specific "repurposeRecipe".
- How will the system determine which recommendation is most appropriate?
Answer: The system monitors the inventory and automatically triggers the specific assigned repurposing recipe whenever a product's Freshness Index falls to 30% or below, provided there is still usable stock remaining.
- Are food safety regulations considered before recommending product reuse?
Answer: Yes. By triggering the repurposing recommendation exactly when the product reaches 30% freshness, the system ensures the product is flagged for reuse while it is still perfectly safe for consumption, well before its actual expiration.

5. Demand forecasting should consider additional variables.
- Will weekends versus weekdays, holidays, weather conditions, seasonal demand, promotions, and local events be included?
Answer: The current prototype focuses strictly on historical sales quantities and a time-based variance coefficient to predict demand. It does not dynamically factor in weather conditions, holidays, or local events. 

6. Clarify production optimization.
- Besides demand forecasts, what other factors are considered?
Answer: The production recommendation logic is calculated as: `(AI Forecasted Demand) - (Current Usable Stock) + (Safety Buffer)`. It explicitly considers the AI prediction, the total usable stock currently in inventory, and a predefined safety stock buffer (currently set to 5 pieces). It does not dynamically calculate raw ingredient availability or oven capacity.

7. Strengthen the novelty.
- What makes BakeWise significantly different from existing bakery management systems?
Answer: While existing systems typically handle only one function (like basic point-of-sale or inventory tracking), BakeWise uniquely integrates demand forecasting, dynamic shelf-life tracking (Freshness Index), and automated product repurposing recommendations into a single, cohesive dashboard tailored for small-to-medium bakeries.

8. Explain the recommendation engine.
- How are recommendations actually generated?
Answer: The recommendation engine relies on a hybrid rule-based approach. The production recommendations are generated through a strict mathematical formula based on the forecast and current stock, while the waste repurposing recommendations are rule-based triggers activated by the 30% freshness threshold.

9. Clarify scalability.
- How will forecasting work when multiple branches have different customer demand patterns?
Answer: The architecture processes the logic locally. For multiple branches, each branch would maintain its own localized database (or database partition), meaning the moving average calculations would automatically run against branch-specific sales records, ensuring the forecast adapts to local demand patterns.
