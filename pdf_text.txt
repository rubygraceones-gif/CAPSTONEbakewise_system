CHAPTER  1:  THE  PROBLEM  AND  ITS  BACKGROUND  
This  chapter  presents  the  background  of  the  study,  project  context,  statement  of  the  problem,  purpose  and  description  of  the  proposed  system,  objectives  of  the  study,  scope  and  limitations,  and  definition  of  terms.  This  chapter  establishes  the  need  for  the  development  of  BakeWise,  an  Artificial  Intelligence-powered  bakery  management  system  designed  to  reduce  bread  waste,  improve  production  planning,  enhance  inventory  monitoring,  and  support  data-driven  decision-making  at  Rose  Bakeshop  –  Lasang  Branch,  Davao  City.  
The  chapter  discusses  the  challenges  experienced  by  bakery  businesses  in  managing  products  with  limited  shelf  life,  maintaining  proper  inventory  levels,  and  determining  accurate  production  quantities.  Through  the  integration  of  Artificial  Intelligence,  Machine  Learning,  and  data  analytics,  BakeWise  aims  to  provide  a  technological  solution  that  assists  bakery  personnel  in  improving  operational  efficiency  and  reducing  unnecessary  product  waste.  
1.1  Project  Context  
1.1.1  Introduction  
The  advancement  of  digital  technologies  has  significantly  transformed  the  way  organizations  manage  operations,  analyze  information,  and  make  business  
=== Page 15 ===
15  
decisions.  Businesses  across  different  industries  continue  to  adopt  computerized  systems  because  traditional  manual  approaches  may  become  inefficient  when  handling  large  amounts  of  operational  information  and  changing  market  conditions.  Digital  transformation  allows  organizations  to  improve  accuracy,  increase  efficiency,  and  develop  better  strategies  through  the  effective  use  of  data.  
One  of  the  technologies  contributing  to  modern  business  improvement  is  Artificial  Intelligence  (AI).  Artificial  Intelligence  refers  to  computer-based  technologies  capable  of  analyzing  information,  identifying  patterns,  generating  predictions,  and  providing  recommendations  that  assist  users  in  decision-making.  Unlike  conventional  information  systems  that  primarily  focus  on  data  storage  and  retrieval,  AI-powered  systems  are  capable  of  interpreting  information  and  producing  meaningful  insights  based  on  historical  and  current  data.  
According  to  the  United  Nations  Environment  Programme  (2024),  food  waste  remains  a  significant  global  concern  because  large  quantities  of  food  are  lost  throughout  different  stages  of  production,  distribution,  and  consumption.  The  issue  affects  not  only  environmental  sustainability  but  also  the  economic  performance  of  businesses  involved  in  food  production  and  service.  The  report  emphasized  that  improved  forecasting,  inventory  management,  and  technological  integration  are  important  
strategies  in  reducing  unnecessary  food  waste.  
The  bakery  industry  is  one  of  the  food  sectors  that  experiences  challenges  related  to  waste  management  because  bakery  products  generally  have  short  shelf  lives.  Bread  products  require  accurate  production  planning  because  producing  quantities  beyond  customer  demand  may  result  in  unsold  products,  while  insufficient  production  may  lead  to  missed  sales  opportunities.  Therefore,  bakery  businesses  need  effective  systems  that  can  analyze  demand  patterns,  monitor  product  conditions,  and  support  production  decisions.  
Machine  Learning  (ML),  a  branch  of  Artificial  Intelligence,  provides  opportunities  for  businesses  to  improve  decision-making  by  learning  from  historical  data.  Machine  Learning  models  can  analyze  previous  sales  records,  inventory  movement,  and  production  information  to  identify  patterns  and  generate  predictions.  In  bakery  operations,  these  capabilities  can  support  demand  forecasting,  inventory  optimization,  and  waste  reduction  strategies  (Hübner  et  al.,  2024).  
BakeWise  applies  these  technological  concepts  by  developing  an  AI-powered  web-based  bakery  management  system  specifically  designed  for  Rose  Bakeshop  –  Lasang  Branch,  Davao  City.  The  system  focuses  on  improving  production  planning,  monitoring  inventory  conditions,  predicting  product  shelf  life,  
=== Page 16 ===
16  
analyzing  waste  patterns,  and  providing  recommendations  that  support  better  bakery  management  decisions.  
1.1.2  Background  of  the  Study  
Rose  Bakeshop  is  a  bakery  establishment  that  provides  various  baked  products,  including  bread,  pastries,  cakes,  and  other  food  products.  For  this  study,  Rose  Bakeshop  –  Lasang  Branch  in  Davao  City  serves  as  the  official  implementation  site  where  BakeWise  will  be  developed,  tested,  evaluated,  and  refined  based  on  actual  operational  requirements.  
Like  many  bakery  businesses,  Rose  Bakeshop  –  Lasang  Branch  faces  operational  challenges  related  to  production  planning,  inventory  monitoring,  and  management  of  unsold  products.  Since  bakery  products  have  limited  shelf  lives,  inaccurate  estimation  of  customer  demand  may  lead  to  excess  production,  unsold  inventory,  and  unnecessary  product  waste.  
Traditional  bakery  operations  often  depend  on  employee  experience  and  manual  estimation  when  determining  production  quantities.  Although  experience  remains  an  important  factor  in  business  operations,  manual  decision-making  may  not  always  consider  multiple  variables  such  as  previous  sales  trends,  customer  purchasing  behavior,  seasonal  demand  changes,  and  historical  waste  patterns.  
Another  challenge  involves  inventory  monitoring.  Without  an  intelligent  monitoring  system,  bakery  personnel  may  need  to  manually  check  product  availability  and  expiration  conditions.  This  process  may  become  difficult  when  managing  multiple  products  with  different  shelf-life  durations.  Delayed  identification  of  near-expiry  products  may  increase  the  possibility  of  product  loss.  
Furthermore,  unsold  bread  products  that  remain  safe  for  consumption  may  not  always  be  maximized  because  businesses  may  lack  systems  capable  of  recommending  possible  repurposing  strategies.  Without  proper  analysis,  potentially  reusable  products  may  become  waste  instead  of  being  transformed  into  alternative  bakery  products.  
The  Department  of  Trade  and  Industry  (2023)  emphasized  that  digital  transformation  provides  opportunities  for  Micro,  Small,  and  Medium  Enterprises  (MSMEs)  to  improve  productivity,  competitiveness,  and  operational  control.  Digital  management  systems  allow  businesses  to  organize  information,  monitor  performance,  and  make  decisions  based  on  accurate  data  rather  than  relying  solely  on  manual  estimation.  
To  address  these  challenges,  the  researchers  propose  BakeWise:  Bread  Waste  Reduction  and  AI-Driven  Knowledge  for  Efficient  Waste  Management,  Inventory  Analytics,  
=== Page 17 ===
17  
Shelf-Life  Prediction,  and  Production  Enhancement.  The  system  integrates  Artificial  Intelligence  and  Machine  Learning  technologies  to  analyze  operational  data  and  provide  recommendations  that  improve  bakery  decision-making.  
1.1.3  Adoption  Feasibility  of  Rose  Bakeshop  
The  adoption  of  BakeWise  at  Rose  Bakeshop  –  Lasang  Branch  is  feasible  because  the  branch  already  performs  daily  operational  activities  that  generate  the  information  required  by  the  system.  Sales  transactions,  production  records,  inventory  monitoring,  and  waste  information  provide  valuable  data  sources  that  can  be  analyzed  by  Artificial  Intelligence.  
The  proposed  system  does  not  require  complete  replacement  of  existing  bakery  procedures.  Instead,  BakeWise  enhances  current  workflows  by  converting  operational  information  into  organized  digital  records.  Existing  business  activities  can  be  improved  through  automated  monitoring,  predictive  analysis,  and  recommendation  generation.

4.8  System  Testing  and  Validation  
System  testing  and  validation  are  important  stages  in  ensuring  that  BakeWise  performs  according  to  the  identified  requirements.  The  purpose  of  testing  is  to  determine  whether  the  developed  system  functions  correctly,  produces  accurate  outputs,  and  provides  an  acceptable  user  experience.  
Testing  will  be  conducted  after  the  development  of  the  system  prototype.  The  researchers  will  evaluate  different  aspects  of  BakeWise,  including  functionality,  usability,  reliability,  performance,  and  security.  
Functional  testing  will  determine  whether  each  system  feature  operates  properly  based  on  its  intended  purpose.  This  includes  testing  user  authentication,  sales  recording,  inventory  monitoring,  production  recording,  Artificial  Intelligence  analysis,  report  generation,  and  dashboard  display.  
Usability  testing  will  evaluate  whether  bakery  personnel  can  easily  understand  and  operate  the  system.  Since  BakeWise  will  be  used  by  employees  with  different  responsibilities,  the  system  interface  must  provide  clear  navigation  and  understandable  functions.  
Performance  testing  will  examine  the  ability  of  BakeWise  to  process  information  efficiently.  This  includes  evaluating  system  response  time,  data  processing  capability,  and  report  generation  performance.  
Security  testing  will  evaluate  the  protection  of  user  accounts  and  business  information.  The  researchers  will  ensure  that  access  to  system  functions  is  controlled  through  authentication  and  assigned  user  permissions.  
Validation  will  involve  gathering  feedback  from  selected  users  of  Rose  Bakeshop  –  Lasang  Rose  Branch.  Their  evaluation  will  determine  whether  the  system  features  address  identified  operational  concerns  and  whether  the  system  is  suitable  for  bakery  management  operations.  
4.8.1  Testing  Strategy  
The  testing  strategy  of  BakeWise  follows  a  systematic  approach  to  verify  that  the  developed  system  satisfies  both  functional  and  quality  requirements.  
The  researchers  will  conduct  module  testing  to  verify  the  performance  of  individual  system  components.  Each  module,  including  sales  management,  inventory  management,  production  management,  waste  analytics,  and  Artificial  Intelligence  functions,  will  be  evaluated  separately  before  being  integrated  into  the  complete  system.  
Integration  testing  will  be  conducted  to  ensure  that  all  system  components  communicate  properly  with  each  other.  This  process  verifies  that  data  entered  from  different  modules  can  be  processed  correctly  and  used  by  Artificial  Intelligence  functions.

System  testing  will  evaluate  the  overall  operation  of  BakeWise  as  a  complete  system.  This  stage  determines  whether  the  system  can  successfully  perform  its  intended  functions  under  actual  operational  conditions.  
User  acceptance  testing  will  be  conducted  with  selected  bakery  personnel  to  determine  whether  BakeWise  meets  user  expectations.  The  feedback  obtained  from  users  will  serve  as  a  basis  for  further  improvements  before  final  implementation.
